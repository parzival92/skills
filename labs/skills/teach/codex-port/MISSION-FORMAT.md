# MISSION.md Format

`MISSION.md` lives at the workspace root and captures why the user is learning this topic.

## Template

```md
# Mission: {Topic}

## Why
{1-3 sentences describing the concrete real-world goal.}

## Success looks like
- {Specific observable outcome}
- {Another specific observable outcome}

## Constraints
- {Time, budget, prior commitments, learning preferences, or other constraints}

## Out of scope
- {Adjacent topics to avoid for now}
```

## Rules

- Keep one mission per workspace.
- Prefer concrete outcomes over abstract understanding.
- Push back on vagueness before teaching.
- Revise the mission when reality shifts.
- Keep the mission short enough to act as a compass.
